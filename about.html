About Us

BC Academy equips talent with practical technology skills to shape tomorrow.

Our Mission

BC Academy equips learners with practical, job-ready technology skills through hands-on training, expert guidance and a supportive learning environment, so every student can build confidence and a career they are proud of.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of technology training in Africa, producing skilled, innovative and ethical professionals who shape the future of their communities and the digital economy.

Our Story

BC Academy started in 2019 as a small training room in Nairobi with one instructor and a few computers. Our first learners had talent but lacked hands-on skills, so we built courses around real projects. Today we train hundreds of learners each year, guided by one belief: practical skills open doors.
